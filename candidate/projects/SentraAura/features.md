# SentraAura — Key Features

- Full Content Asset Graph with lineage queries — every published clip
  traceable back to its source research/video
- AI Clipping Engine: 8-factor composite scoring (semantic importance,
  narrative completeness, retention potential, emotional intensity,
  novelty, hook strength, quotability, contextual boundaries)
- Quality & Safety Gates (§9) with a composite quality score before
  anything auto-publishes
- Fault-tolerant automation: explicit failure-scenario/recovery matrix,
  job/workflow state machine, post-publish compensation & rollback
- Human Control Plane: risk-based approval policy, override logging,
  and a "Reasoning Artifact" — a decision-transparency record for why
  the system made a given autonomous choice
- Experimentation Engine with a statistical framework and explicit
  false-conclusion-prevention rules for winner selection
