- Full source-provenance traceability table — every mechanism from the
  4 source documents is accounted for in exactly one layer, nothing
  dropped, nothing duplicated
- Explicit named terminal states per layer (not a generic "signal
  rejected") — SYSTEM DORMANT, HIBERNATING, MONITOR MODE, ABORT, ORDER
  CANCELED, GAP DISCARDED, KILL SWITCH

  through fills, dynamic re-sizing, and the TP cascade
- Explicit failure-mode matrix and a state-precedence hierarchy for
  resting orders vs. open positions
