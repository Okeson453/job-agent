# AegisOS — Challenges & Solutions

**The core tension:** AegisShare's zero-knowledge guarantee cannot relax
just because AegisTriage's agents want to reason about vault content —
most "combined" platforms let convenience quietly erode the "secure"
label. AegisOS's answer is structural (CI fitness functions), not
conventional (developer discipline), because discipline alone erodes
within ~6 months on similar systems.

**Unified UX problem:** AegisShare's aesthetic (Playfair Display,
JetBrains Mono, gold admin accents, Bloomberg/Palantir-inspired) and
AegisTriage's aesthetic (IBM Plex Mono, cyan tactical, SOC-cockpit
scanline texture) need a genuinely new shared identity, not a
compromise between the two.

**Market/positioning risk:** the pitch depends on the buyer having
*both* a SOC-operations problem and a data-governance problem
simultaneously — a narrower buyer profile than either product alone,
traded for a much higher willingness-to-pay ("regulatory risk
mitigation" pricing vs. commoditized "SOC tool" pricing).
